A plain-language overview of the information the Ambassador Cre8tive website collects and why.

Last updated15 November 2026Ask a privacy question ↗

What this website collects

When you send a project brief, the website collects the contact and project details you choose to provide. When you request creative notes, it stores your email as a pending request until you confirm it from your inbox. Confirmed subscribers can receive the notes until they unsubscribe.

Why it is used

Project-brief information is used to review and respond to your enquiry, manage a possible client relationship, and improve the way Ambassador Cre8tive communicates about its services. Confirmed newsletter emails are used to send occasional creative notes. The public website does not publish your private brief or newsletter email address.

Where it is stored

The site is configured to use Firebase for protected project, enquiry, and subscription records. When configured by Ambassador Cre8tive, Vercel and Resend support secure website functions and email delivery. Website analytics may record non-sensitive interaction events to understand site use; these events are not designed to include your name, email address, or project message.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe using the link in a creative-notes email. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you have provided, email mobolajiolakunle8@gmail.com. Ambassador Cre8tive should review operational retention practices regularly and respond in line with applicable requirements.

Before launch

This page describes the current website workflow, not legal advice. Before collecting production leads or sending marketing email, Ambassador Cre8tive should confirm its final privacy, retention, consent, and cross-border-processing obligations with an appropriate adviser.
